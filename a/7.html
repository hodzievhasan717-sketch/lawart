Legal Stability of Investment Incentives and Investor Protection in Uzbekistan's Special Economic Zones

YuanLingYun
Lawart, 26.11.2026

Аннотация

Special economic zones (SEZs) constitute a central component of Uzbekistan's strategy to attract foreign direct investment, offering tax preferences, customs benefits and regulatory advantages to qualifying investors. However, the effectiveness of SEZ incentives depends not only on their initial availability but also on their stability over the life of the investment and the legal protection available when incentives are modified, withdrawn or terminated. This article examines the legal framework governing investment incentives in Uzbekistan's SEZs, analysing the duration and conditions of incentives, the legal consequences of their modification or termination, the dispute resolution mechanisms available to affected investors, and the adequacy of existing protections. The analysis focuses on the Law on Special Economic Zones (Law No.-604 of 17 February 2020), the Tax Code (Law No.-599 of 30 December 2019) and relevant implementing instruments, with particular attention to Article 42 of the SEZ Law, which provides a form of statutory stabilisation for participants. The article identifies key legal issues - including the scope and limitations of statutory stabilisation, the legal consequences of eligibility withdrawal, the interaction between domestic and international remedies, and the impact of the 2026 profit tax regime change - and proposes measures to improve the stability, transparency and predictability of SEZ investment incentives.
